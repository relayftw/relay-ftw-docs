Supported Watches & Devices

Relay FTW requires hardware running Wear OS 3.0 or greater. This version of Wear OS allows for the high-performance Bluetooth communication required to relay signals instantly on the field.

Critical Compatibility Note

The Samsung Galaxy Watch 3 and older models (Watch Active 2, Gear S3) run on "Tizen OS" and are NOT supported by Relay FTW.

Supported Devices (Wear OS 3.0+)

Samsung Galaxy Series

Samsung is our most recommended hardware for durability on the field.

  • Galaxy Watch 7 / Ultra
  • Galaxy Watch 6 & 6 Classic
  • Galaxy Watch 5 & 5 Pro
  • Galaxy Watch 4 & 4 Classic
  • Galaxy Watch FE

Google Pixel Series

Known for excellent haptic feedback (vibrations), which helps players feel signals during loud games.

  • Pixel Watch 3
  • Pixel Watch 2
  • Pixel Watch (Original)

Other Verified Models

  • OnePlus Watch 2 (Excellent battery life for long tournaments)
  • Mobvoi TicWatch Pro 5
  • Xiaomi Watch 2 / Pro

Unsupported Hardware

These devices do not have the software architecture required to run the Relay FTW receiver app.

  • All Apple Watches (iOS is not compatible)
  • Samsung Galaxy Watch 3 and older (Tizen OS)
  • Garmin / Fitbit Devices
  • Fossil Gen 5 and older

Hardware Tips for Coaches

Battery Management

For long tournament days, we recommend the Galaxy Watch 5 Pro or Galaxy Watch Ultra. They offer the best battery life for constant Bluetooth use.
