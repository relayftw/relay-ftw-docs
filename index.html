Relay For The Win

Relay FTW is a high-performance communication tool designed for the heat of the game. It allows coaches to broadcast instant messages to players directly on the field using seamless Bluetooth technology. Whether you need to organize your strategy with custom tabs, message specific player groups, or send a "single-shot" alert, Relay FTW ensures your team stays synchronized in real-time.
